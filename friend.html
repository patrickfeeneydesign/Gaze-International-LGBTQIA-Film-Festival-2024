    Festival Friend

    With Benefits...

    In our fourth decade, GAZE continues to bring strangers and friends together for breathtaking, mind-bending and life - affirming cinematic experiences.
    Buy Now

    We need your support. Becoming a Festival Friend is one of the best ways you can support the festival and our team. GAZE operates as an independent festival without a title sponsor and this independence allows us to programme bold, groundbreaking films that push boundaries and reflect the diversity and richness of LGBTQIA storytelling from around the world. Your patronage brings invaluable resources to our 2026 programme, helping us expand the breadth of films and experiences we can offer audiences. At a time when LGBTQIA+ communities continue to face prejudice and challenges, GAZE remains committed to creating a vibrant, welcoming space that celebrates cinema while fostering connection, freedom, and joy. By becoming a Festival Friend again, you'll not only enjoy a host of benefits and perks, but you'll also be directly supporting a community-focused festival that champions visibility, creativity, and inclusivity.

    FESTIVAL FRIEND - €130
    Your Festival Friend package includes:

    - One ticket for both our Opening and Closing Gala films plus pre-film receptions.
    - Two complimentary tickets during the festival.*
    - Recognition on screen and on our website.
    - Invitations to exclusive Festival Friends events.

    DOUBLE FRIEND - €200
    All the benefits of the Festival Friends package plus:

    - An extra ticket to bring a friend, partner or date to both our Opening and Closing Gala films.
    - Two additional complimentary tickets during the festival.*
    - Recognition on screen and on our website.
    - Invitations to exclusive Festival Friends events.

    BEST FESTIVAL FRIEND - €350
    All the benefits of the Double Friend package plus:

    - A Festival Pass which gives you complimentary ticket to every screening you want to see during the festival.*
    - Access all areas! Your support gets you access to all in-person online screenings and invitations to special events throughout the festival.

    *Tickets must be booked in advance to ensure availability, including special events.

    In addition to the Festival Friend benefits above, your patronage also gives you access to the GAZE Festival Launch and special year-round events. Previous events include premieres such as: All of Us Strangers with Andrew Haigh, Andrew Scott and Paul Mescal in attendance, the Lesbian Visibility Week special preview of Love Lies Bleeding, and last year's star-studded ensemble Honey Don't!

    Donate to GAZE

    If you're not in a position to join us in person this year, you can still make a meaningful difference by making a donation. Every contribution, no matter the size, helps us continue this work.

    GAZE operates with a small, dedicated team working with limited resources and every contribution goes directly into the festival itself: onto our screens, into our events, and into the community we build each year.
